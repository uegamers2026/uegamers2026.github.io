Season Contest Rules

The official rules of the monthly Season contest and its Google Play gift card prizes.

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · First season: November 2026
  • Free to enterNo purchase is needed, and nothing you buy in the game helps you win.
  • A game of skillThe best Season-mode runs win. No luck, draws or betting.
  • India onlyFor players in India, because Google Play gift cards work only in the country they're bought for.
  • 3 winners every monthGoogle Play gift cards: ₹1,000 for 1st, ₹500 for 2nd and ₹300 for 3rd, shown in the game.

1Organiser

The Season contest ("the contest") is run by UE Gamers, the developer of the mobile game Avalanche Paws: Snow Escape ("the game"), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Contact: uegamers2026@gmail.com.

Google is not a sponsor of this contest and is not involved in it in any way. Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC.

2Who can enter

  • Anyone who lives in India and plays the game from India, with a leaderboard nickname in the game.
  • Winners must be 18 or older, or have permission from a parent or guardian to take part and receive the prize.
  • Entry is free. Playing Season mode enters you; there is nothing to sign up for or pay.
  • UE Gamers and its family members can't win.
  • One entry per person. Each leaderboard nickname belongs to one player.

3The season and Season mode

  • A season is one calendar month, from midnight on the 1st to midnight at the end of the month, India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30). The first season is November 2026.
  • The contest uses Season mode on the game's home screen and its own monthly 🏆 Season leaderboard.
  • Season mode is the same for everyone. Hero perks, bought boosts, extra lives and upgrades don't work in it, and power-ups last their basic time. You can be revived twice per run, only by watching an optional ad, never with diamonds or anything bought.
  • Only runs started while your phone is online count. A run that starts offline isn't counted.

4Becoming eligible

  • To be eligible for a prize you must play the game for at least 30 minutes on at least 15 different days of the season. Play in any mode counts toward these minutes. A day runs from midnight to midnight IST.
  • Play time is measured by our game server's clock from the start and end of each run, not by your phone's clock.
  • Your eligible Season-mode runs must be played from India.
  • After each Season run the game shows your place on the board and how many more days you need.

5Choosing the winners

  • After the season ends (from the 2nd of the next month), all eligible players are ranked by their single best Season-mode run distance in that season.
  • If two runs have the same distance, the run that was made first ranks higher.
  • The 3 highest-ranked eligible players win. The next 10 are reserves, in order. If a winner is disqualified or can't accept the prize, the players below move up one place.
  • We check every winner's runs and play time before the prizes are given out.
  • If fewer than 3 players are eligible, fewer prizes are given that month.

6Prizes

  • Each winner gets one Google Play gift card (India), bought from an authorised seller: 1st place ₹1,000, 2nd place ₹500, 3rd place ₹300.
  • The gift card code appears in the winner's game ("🎉 You won!") the next time the home screen opens, usually within 15 days after the season ends. It stays in the Season screen for 60 days after it's first shown.
  • The code is redeemed in the Google Play Store app (Menu → Redeem code) and can only be used on a Google Play account in India. Google Play's gift card terms apply.
  • Prizes can't be exchanged for cash, transferred or swapped for something else.
  • If a code doesn't work, email us within 60 days of receiving it and we'll help.
  • If you uninstall the game or remove your nickname before your code is shown, write to us from the game's email link with your nickname.

7Fair play

We may disqualify a player, and take back a prize that hasn't been redeemed yet, if they:

  • use a modified game, cheats, bots, scripts, emulators or anything that changes how the game or its clock works;
  • use several nicknames, devices or accounts to enter more than once, or share an account;
  • attack, overload or try to fool our servers, or report false play time or distances;
  • use an offensive nickname, or break the game's rules or Google Play's terms.

Our decision about eligibility, rankings and winners is final.

8General terms

  • Taxes: each prize is worth less than ₹10,000. Winners are responsible for any tax that applies to them.
  • Your data: to run the contest we use your nickname, play time per day, run distances and the country your Season runs are sent from, as described in our Privacy Policy. A winner's nickname may appear on the Season board. You can remove your data at any time (Delete your data), but you then leave the contest.
  • Changes: we may change these rules, the prize or the number of winners for future seasons, or end the contest, by updating this page. We won't change a season's rules to a player's disadvantage after that season has started, unless the law or Google Play's policies require it.
  • Problems: we aren't responsible for runs or play time lost to network problems, phone problems or service outages.
  • Law: these rules are governed by the laws of India. The courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.
  • Where the contest isn't allowed by law, it is void.
