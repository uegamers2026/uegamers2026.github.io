How it plays

Swipe. Jump.
Don't look back.

Three lanes, one sled and a mountain that won't stop falling. Swipe to switch lanes, jump logs and penguins, slide under low branches and owls, and keep the coins coming.

  • Endless runner
  • One-thumb controls
  • Free to play
  • No sign-up
🏔️

Outrun the avalanche

Dodge trees, rocks and snow animals. Slow down and the wall of snow catches up, so keep moving.

🛷

Ramps & tricks

Hit a snow ramp, fly through a trail of coins and land a full sled spin for bonus points.

🔥

Combos & Fury

Chain coins into big combos, then unleash Avalanche Fury and smash through everything.

🧲

Power-ups & boosts

Magnets, shields and more on the slope, plus boosts to take into your next run.

Meet the locals

Snow animals share the slope.

Now and then one wanders into your lane and stops. Dodge it, jump it, slide under it, or tap to throw a snowball and watch it hop out of the way. No animals are harmed.

YetiDodge

Big and fluffy, and it plants itself right in your lane.

ReindeerDodge

Grazing in the lane. Look for the glowing red nose.

PenguinJump

Waddles across, about knee high. Hop over it.

Arctic foxJump

Trots out and sits down, low to the snow.

Snowy owlSlide

Hovers at head height. Duck under it.
