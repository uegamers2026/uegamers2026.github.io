Delete your data

Avalanche Paws: Snow Escape · by UE Gamers

1How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to uegamers2026@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – Avalanche Paws”.
  2. Include the Google account email or Play Games name you use in the game, so we can find your records.
  3. We confirm your request by email and complete it within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

2What gets deleted

  • Game progress (coins, diamonds, unlocked items, settings, best scores) is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the game, or Settings → Apps → Avalanche Paws → Storage → Clear data, deletes it immediately.
  • Wipeout sound recordings never leave your phone and are deleted with the game’s data.
  • Leaderboard scores and sign-in are held by Google Play Games. On request we ask Google to remove your scores from our leaderboards. You can also delete your whole Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.

3What may be kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required for payments, refunds and tax law. We don’t hold your payment details.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google’s policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

We don’t run our own servers or create accounts, so beyond the above we hold no personal data about you. See the full privacy policy.
