Avalanche Paws: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Avalanche Paws ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Developer: UE Gamers. Contact: uegamers2026@gmail.com.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress: coins, best score, unlocked and equipped items, settings. Saved locally on your device and never sent to us.
  • Wipeout sound (optional microphone use): if you choose to record a wipeout sound, the game asks for microphone permission and records up to 3 seconds of audio. The recording is stored only on your device, is used only to play back in the game, and is never uploaded, shared or sold. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Use default meow (or Add Sound → Back to the default meow), or by clearing the app's data. Microphone access is never used for anything else.

2. Information collected by third-party services

ServicePurposeData it may collectPolicy
Google AdMobShowing ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded) Advertising ID / device identifiers, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland (and in some US states), the game shows a consent form before personalized ads. You can change your choice any time from Settings (⚙) → Privacy settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Microphone: optional. You can deny or revoke the permission in your device's settings; the game works fully without it.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes all locally stored data, including any recording. To ask about data held by the services above, contact us at the email above.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
