This policy explains what information the mobile game Avalanche Paws ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Developer: UE Gamers. Contact: uegamers2026@gmail.com.

1Information stored on your device

  • Game progress: coins, diamonds, snowflakes, your best distances, unlocked and equipped items, purchases you made, your player level, daily missions, the Goal Ladder and Journey, your Snow Village, season pass level, trophies, mystery chests, upgrades and boosts, and settings. Saved on your device (your best distances also go to the leaderboards described below, if you use them).
  • Backups of your game progress (so a reinstall or a new phone gets it back, including what you bought): the game keeps copies of this game progress (including the purchase receipts' IDs it has already granted) in up to three places: (1) with your leaderboard entry on our game server (Cloudflare, see below), (2) in your Google Play Games account (Saved Games) if you are signed in, and (3) in Android's own backup of the app in your Google account. The copies are used only to give your progress back. The wipeout sound recording is never included.
  • Wipeout sound (optional microphone use): if you choose to record a wipeout sound, the game asks for microphone permission and records up to 3 seconds of audio. The recording is stored only on your device, is used only to play back in the game, and is never uploaded, shared or sold. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Use default meow (or Add Sound → Back to the default meow), or by clearing the app's data. Microphone access is never used for anything else.

3Your choices

  • Consent and opt-out: if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. If you live in a US state with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. You can change either choice at any time from Settings (⚙) → Privacy settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Microphone: optional. You can deny or revoke the permission in your device's settings; the game works fully without it.
  • Purchases: all purchases are optional and handled by Google Play. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy; use Store → Restore purchases to get premium items back on a new device.
  • Leaderboards: the nickname leaderboard is optional. You can change your nickname or delete it together with all your scores at any time in Settings → Leaderboard nickname → Remove me from the leaderboard. Please don't use your real full name. You can report a rude nickname or village name with the 🚩 button. Friends are only added with a friend code you share (or your invite link); you can remove a friend at any time in the village's 👥 Friends tab. For Google Play Games, you can sign out or hide your gameplay activity in the Play Games app at any time.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes all locally stored data, including any recording. To ask about data held by the services above, see Delete your data or contact us at the email below.

4Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.
